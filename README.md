Hlw there grinders, now every RNG drop feels like an achievement, more than just a chat message!      
I will make sure that your dopamine receptors actually pump dopamine when you drop a Rng xd


# Features
1. 150+ items (slayers, dungeons, dragon armours, fishing, farming, mining, dyes, pets and more) have their own animation.
2. Every item animation can be toggled based on your liking (dyes and dragon armours have a common toggle)
3. The item pops up with a custom sound (sound inspired from subway surfers mystery box opening sound)
4. A pasted fake drop message OR other players drops doesn’t trigger the animation for you, ONLY YOURS <3
5. Has a Snow themed UI, cuz why not
6. Pretty much everything is configurable - (flash, background dim, sound and volume, animation length, item size, spin speed, ray strength, and queue limit)
7. Very lightweight, hence mobile players can use it too without any lag issues.
